NYC Board Summary: 20 Use Cases on the Road to Dallas

Board Summary· 20 Use Cases · 118 Votes · 5 Working Groups· Peter Campbell, Head of Collaborative Working Groups

On October 27 in New York, ONUG Executive Board members presented their use cases for 2027 and voted on them. Nineteen came in the meeting packet and a twentieth arrived as a separate handout. The Board cast 118 votes, and from them came five working groups for 2027. Each one now heads to the AI Networking Summit Dallas, May 12–13, 2027, under the theme “Proving the AI Infrastructure Fabric.”

3. From the NYC Summit to Dallas

Build → Prove

The Board met on October 27, the day before the AI Networking Summit New York (October 28–29, PENN District). New York was about building the network for enterprise AI. Dallas is about proving it. The tagline and the five tracks carry over, so the working groups’ 2027 work lands on the same stage it was previewed on.

Six Months to Dallas

Each group’s leads are now choosing a path: what to publish and demonstrate in May, and what moves to the second half of 2027.

Proving the AI Infrastructure Fabric

Dallas asks for proof. Several Board asks can already be shown: agent identity revoked mid-task, fleet-wide stop, and a signed journal run today in the WG1 reference implementation. A WG2 member has proposed a live demo, a slow bank teller transaction diagnosed and fixed across domains, that would put four WG2 use cases (#2, #10, #12, #18) and WG1’s controls on stage in one story.
