This briefing prepares WG2 (Autonomous Operations) leaders for 2027 planning and the road to the AI Networking Summit Dallas (May 12–13, 2027, Hyatt Regency Frisco). WG2 enters 2027 with the second-highest board vote concentration (37 of 118 votes cast, 31%) across five absorbed board use cases—including two of the top four proposals on the entire ONUG Board: UC #2 (Cross-Domain Telemetry & Incident Attribution, 15 votes, Rank #2) and UC #17 (Enterprise Network Knowledge Agent, 11 votes, Rank #3). In parallel, as WG3 refocuses on AI Agents as Insider Threats, WG2 onboards the ratified v0.6 SOC Persona (Decide Gate) alongside its v0.6 NOC Persona (Verify Gate) on the shared v0.6 skeleton ratified October 15, 2026.

Why the SOC Persona Joins the NOC Persona Inside WG2

None of the 20 October board submissions asked for an AI-assisted SOC, and WG3 is refocusing on AI Agents as Insider Threats (anchored by UC #5). Because WG2 and WG3 built their ratified v0.6 reference architectures (October 15, 2026) on the exact same two-wrapper, 6-step loop skeleton, moving the v0.6 SOC Persona into WG2 preserves the Governed Autonomy v3 board paper’s two-domain proof: “the next domain should onboard without new enforcement code” (Focus Area #8: Verify Gate alongside Decide Gate).

1. WG2’s Updated 2027 Scope: What Continues & What Folds In

Continuing Working Group

WG2’s ratified v0.6 Autonomous Infrastructure Reference Architecture (October 15, 2026) remains the spine of the group: two outer wrappers (Strategic Intent & Policy on top; WG1’s Access Enforcement & Audit below) framing the 6-step closed loop (1 Detect → 2 Diagnose → 3 Propose → 4 Verify Gate → 5 Execute → 6 Observe & Learn). For 2027, WG2 organizes around three concrete tracks:

Workstream 1 · Steps 1–2 (Detect & Diagnose)

Cross-Domain Telemetry & Incident Attribution

Anchored by UC #2 (15 votes · Rank #2 overall):

Correlates telemetry across carrier underlay (DWDM), enterprise overlay (SD-WAN/EVPN), cloud black-box gateways, and internal DNS/endpoints to end NOC “innocence proving” during brownouts where every silo reports green in isolation.

v0.6 Fit: Steps 1–2 + Focus Area #6 (Sources of Truth)
Workstream 2 · Rung 1 + Step 4 (Verify Gate)

Network Digital Twin & Pre-Change Verify Gate

Combines UC #17 (11v), UC #18 (4v), and UC #12 (2v) — 17 votes total:

UC #17 defines the Rung 1 Network Knowledge Agent explaining design intent; UC #18 defines the Step 4 Pre-Change Validation Agent (design compliance + execution-time health check); and UC #12 extends Containerlab + NetBox into an open digital-twin validation harness.

v0.6 Fit: Rung 1 (noc-observer) + Step 4 (Verify Gate) + Focus Area #9
Workstream 3 · Focus Area #8 Convergence

Onboarding the Ratified v0.6 SOC Persona

Preserving the Two-Domain Proof (Verify Gate + Decide Gate):

Brings the ratified v0.6 SOC Persona (1 Detect → 2 Investigate → 3 Propose → 4 Decide Gate → 5 Respond → 6 Validate & Learn, plus the observe-mode honored invariant) into WG2 alongside the NOC Persona, directly operationalizing Focus Area #8 (NOC + SOC convergence).

v0.6 Fit: Shared v0.6 Skeleton + Focus Area #8

3. Two Personas on One Skeleton: NOC (Verify Gate) + SOC (Decide Gate)

Unified v0.6 Architecture

Housing both ratified v0.6 personas inside WG2 keeps the two-domain architecture intact and demonstrates how two operational domains share the same 6-step loop while differing only at Step 4 (physics vs. adversary):

v0.6 Loop Stage WG2 NOC Persona (Infrastructure Operations) WG2 SOC Persona (Security Operations · Onboarded from WG3 v0.6)
Top Wrapper Strategic Intent & Policy: Availability SLOs, change windows, design intent (UC #17). Strategic Intent & Policy: Risk appetite, containment policy, observe-mode rules.
Step 1 1 Detect — Ingest wire, flow, BGP/EVPN, DWDM, and cloud gateway telemetry (UC #2). 1 Detect — Ingest SIEM/XDR, DNS, identity, and WG3 insider-threat behavioral detections.
Step 2 2 Diagnose — Cross-domain root-cause attribution across underlay/overlay/cloud (UC #2, #17). 2 Investigate — Correlate blast radius, credential lineage, and MITRE ATT&CK / ATLAS chain.
Step 3 3 Propose — Draft candidate config diff, traffic drain, or failover plan + rollback plan. 3 Propose — Draft containment action (quarantine host, revoke SVID/token, or continue observing).
Step 4 (The Gate) 4 Verify Gate (Physics & Topology Gate): Validate in Digital Twin (UC #12) + pre-change compliance & health check (UC #18). Question: Will this break the network? 4 Decide Gate (Adversarial & Intelligence Gate): Weigh containment against intelligence value. Enforces the observe-mode honored invariant (watching an intruder can outweigh tipping them off).
Step 5 5 Execute — Push bounded change via MCP gateway with ephemeral OpenBao credentials (noc-operator). 5 Respond — Execute bounded containment or SVID/credential revocation via WG1 control plane.
Step 6 6 Observe & Learn — Verify post-change SLOs; record outcome to WG1 immudb journal and 5-SLI scorecard. 6 Validate & Learn — Confirm containment efficacy; feed post-incident learning into WG1 scorecard.
Bottom Wrapper WG1 Access Enforcement & Audit Substrate: SPIFFE/SPIRE SVIDs, OpenBao credential injection, MCP tool gateway, immudb WORM audit journal, and the 5-SLI Rung Promotion Scorecard.

4. How WG2 Aligns With & Complements WG1, WG3, WG4, and WG5

Cross-Group Architecture

WG2 sits at the operational center of the 2027 ONUG portfolio, consuming WG1’s governance substrate and coordinating directly with WG3, WG4, and WG5:

Sibling Group How WG2 & That Group Complement Each Other Concrete Technical Handoff
WG1 Continue
Agentic Control Plane		 WG1 owns the Bottom Wrapper; WG2 owns the Operational Personas & 6-Step Loop. WG2 does not rebuild agent identity, secret storage, or audit logging; it runs on WG1. WG2’s noc-observer (Rung 1), noc-operator (Rung 2), and SOC personas authenticate via WG1 SPIRE SVIDs, execute through WG1’s MCP gateway, and emit Step 4/Step 6 outcomes into WG1’s immudb journal to compute the 5-SLI promotion scorecard.
WG3 Updated
AI Security: AI Agents as Insider Threats		 WG2 operates and defends infrastructure; WG3 investigates AI agents themselves as insider threats. 1. Attribution (UC #2 ↔ UC #20 Scenario 5): WG2 isolates whether an anomaly is an underlay/overlay/cloud fault vs. an agent-induced incident.
2. Containment: When WG3 detects an in-policy misused or shadow agent (UC #5), it feeds the alert into WG2’s SOC Decide Gate.
WG4 New
AI WAN		 Shared network-operator audience, clean layer split: WG2 = Operations Plane; WG4 = Transport Data Plane. WG4 defines AI WAN transport requirements and reference architecture (semantic data reduction in flight and deterministic sync between edge inference and central training, UC #1). WG2 runs autonomous NOC operations and telemetry attribution (UC #2) over those WAN links.
WG5 New
Post-Quantum Security for AI Infrastructure		 Joint split of UC #10 (10 votes): WG2 owns autonomous 24/7 operations and digital-twin validation; WG5 owns post-quantum readiness and key resiliency. WG2’s Digital Twin & Pre-Change Verify Gate (UC #12/#18) serves as the testbed to simulate WG5’s post-quantum certificate/key rotations (testing ML-DSA/ML-KEM packet fragmentation, handshake latency, and KMS outage recovery) before production rollout.

5. Input Needed from WG2 Leads for the Road to Dallas

May 12–13, 2027 Target

To prepare WG2’s 2027 deliverables and demonstrations for the AI Networking Summit Dallas (May 12–13, 2027, Hyatt Regency Frisco), we need WG2 leadership input on five questions:

Decision Area Context & Options Question for WG2 Leads
1. Structuring the Two Flagship 2027 NOC Workstreams The five absorbed board use cases cluster naturally into (A) Cross-Domain Telemetry & Incident Attribution (UC #2, 15 votes, Steps 1–2) and (B) Network Knowledge Agent, Pre-Change Verify Gate & Digital Twin (UC #17 + #18 + #12 = 17 votes, Rung 1 + Step 4). Should WG2 organize into these two drafting/demo sub-teams for Dallas, and which current WG2 vendor leads (e.g., CodiLime, cPacket, NetBrain, BlueCat, Aviatrix, Cisco, PalC, Zentera) will anchor each?
2. Extending onug-plane for UC #17 & UC #18 Today onug-plane runs a 3-node Arista cEOS topology in Containerlab with NetBox and two agents (noc-observer at Rung 1, noc-operator at Rung 2). What inputs should we add to noc-observer to demonstrate UC #17 (design intent analysis) and to Step 4 to demonstrate UC #18 (pre-change compliance + execution-window health gate) live at Dallas?
3. Onboarding the v0.6 SOC Persona & Focus Area #8 Moving the ratified v0.6 SOC Persona (Decide Gate + observe-mode honored) into WG2 puts NOC and SOC side by side on the same v0.6 skeleton. How should WG2 structure the SOC Persona track and resolve the open v0.6 design question on cross-domain NOC↔SOC read-access authority?
4. Engaging the 4 Board Submitters as Co-Authors Four board members submitted the five use cases absorbed into WG2 (#2, #10, #12, #17, and #18). How should we pair each board submitter with WG2’s engineering leads to co-author the 2027 specifications and present on the Dallas stage?
5. Defining the WG2 ↔ WG4 and WG2 ↔ WG5 Handshakes WG2 shares a network-operator audience with WG4 (AI WAN) and splits UC #10 with WG5 (PQC). Who from WG2 will serve as liaison to WG4 (to keep the Ops Plane vs. WAN Data Plane boundary crisp) and to WG5 (to run PQC migration dry-runs in WG2’s Digital Twin)?
