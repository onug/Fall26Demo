3. Where the Vote and the Triage Differ

For Board Discussion

UC #11 was set aside but received 8 votes. The triage recommends a briefing only, because structured English inside prompts conflicts with WG1’s rule that governance is enforced outside the prompt. It is tied for sixth with UC #4 and UC #5, so the board may want to revisit that recommendation.

WG3 rests on one direct sponsoring use case. No submission asks for an AI-assisted SOC, so the SOC persona design moves into WG2 (which shares its v0.6 skeleton) and WG3 is refocused on AI agents as insider threats, anchored by UC #5 (shadow and endpoint agents, 8 votes) and supported by agent incident attribution (UC #20 scenario 5 and the agent side of UC #2). No other submission sponsors it directly.

Agent identity stays in WG1. It is the board’s strongest theme, led by UC #15 (18 votes), and all of it except UC #5 is already WG1 or WG5 work.

WG5 has the most support of the new groups. It has 16 votes, mostly from UC #16 (9) and half of UC #10. The two PQC submissions from John Buselli received 1 vote (UC #8) and 0 votes (UC #7).

WG4 rests on one use case. AI WAN has UC #1’s 7 votes and no other submission behind it.

Folding assurance into WG1 makes it the largest group. UC #19 and UC #14 (5 votes between them) no longer form their own group, and UC #4 now goes wholly to WG1, bringing it to 42 votes.
