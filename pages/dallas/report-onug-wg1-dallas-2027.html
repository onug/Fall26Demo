1. WG1 Charter

Continuing Working Group

WG1 defines the shared control layer that every enterprise AI agent runs through. It sets who the agent is, what it may do, where its model calls go, and what record it leaves. All of it is enforced outside the agent, so the next domain can onboard without new enforcement code. The charter rests on the four components of the v0.2 Unified Architecture, ratified July 28, 2026:

ComponentWhat It Does
1. Agent Trust FabricGives each agent a short-lived, task-scoped identity that can be revoked at any time.
2. Registry, Personas & BOMRecords what each agent is and what it is allowed to do, step by step.
3. Runtime SupervisionEnforces policy at the gateway, stops agents fleet-wide, and writes a tamper-evident journal.
4. Private Open RouterRoutes model calls under cost, residency, and safety policy.

3. The Road to Dallas

May 12–13, 2027 · Hyatt Regency Frisco

The AI Networking Summit Dallas 2027 runs May 12–13 at the Hyatt Regency Frisco. This year’s theme asks for proof, and that is what WG1 has been building since its architecture was ratified.

Proving the AI Infrastructure Fabric

“Build the Foundation. Enable the Fabric. Power the AI Future.”

The AI Security track calls for “building security into the fabric itself; not bolted onto the network after the fact.” WG1’s charter is that same argument: enforcement outside the agent, built once, shared by every domain.

4. Potential Paths Forward

Decision for WG1 Leads

All three tracks are 2027 work. The choice is what WG1 publishes and demonstrates in Dallas, and what moves to the second half of 2027.

ONUG leadership is considering another working demo for Dallas. A demo can go with any of the three paths, and each card shows what it would demonstrate. “Running today” means already demonstrated in the WG1 reference implementation.

Path 1 · 21 of 42 votes

Identity First

Dallas: Track A only. Publish the agent identity and per-step authority spec.

For: Puts the Board’s #1 use case first. Identity and fleet-wide stop already run in the reference implementation.

Against: No answer in Dallas to the evidence ask (UC #4, 8 votes).

Demo: An agent gets a short-lived, task-scoped identity, has it revoked mid-task, and the whole fleet stops on command. Running today.

Path 2 · 34 of 42 votes

Identity + Evidence

Dallas: Tracks A and B. Identity spec plus the signed compliance journal and promotion scorecard.

For: Covers 81% of WG1’s votes. Identity, credential isolation, and the signed journal are already demonstrated.

Against: The scorecard (UC #19) and bypass test harness (UC #14) are not built yet.

Demo: Path 1, plus the signed journal producing proof a regulator can check. Running today. Showing the scorecard live means building it before May.

Path 3 · 42 of 42 votes

All Three Tracks

Dallas: Tracks A, B, and C in parallel, adding model routing, sovereignty, and regulatory mappings.

For: Every WG1 use case gets a Dallas deliverable.

Against: Splits a small group across nine use cases in six months. Track C has the least Board weight.

Demo: Path 2, plus model calls routed under budget and residency policy (running today) and a call blocked on jurisdiction (not built).

Questions for WG1 Leads

1. PathWhich path does WG1 take to Dallas?
2. DemoIf ONUG runs a WG1 demo in Dallas, do we commit to it, and does it show only what runs today or include something new built before May?
3. Board AdvisorsEach Board submitter will be invited to co-author their track or serve on it as a Board Advisor to WG1. Who on the leads team makes each invitation, and how often do Board Advisors meet with the group?
4. LiaisonsWho from WG1 works with WG5 on post-quantum agent identity, and with WG3 on the journal fields its investigators need?
