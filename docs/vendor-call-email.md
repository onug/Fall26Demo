**Subject:** The Fall 2026 challenge: show us your answer, in your lane

Colleagues,

On 28 October in New York, the AI Networking Summit opens with a demo called *One Control Plane, Every Domain*. It shows a company doing everything right, a managed registry, scans, a cooling-off hold, and still losing its fabric, its identities and its backups to a poisoned artifact that an agent pulled on schedule. Then it shows the same company with an agentic control plane in place, across three domains: the control plane itself, autonomous infrastructure, and the AI-enabled SOC.

The keynote ends by pointing the room at you. **The challenge is to show how your product answers it, in one lane.**

**Three lanes, matching the working groups.** Pick the one where you are strongest; nobody is expected to cover all three.

1. **WG1 · Agentic Control Plane.** Supervise agents from outside: identity, provenance, personas, runtime drift, the kill switch, the journal.
2. **WG2 · Autonomous Infrastructure.** Let agents run the fabric with every write through the Verify Gate: change-stop, dry-run, blast radius, rollback.
3. **WG3 · AI-Enabled SOC.** Detect-to-decide in seconds, the Decide Gate, containment that preserves evidence.

**What you submit.** A five-to-ten-minute MP4 screen capture of your response, using the working groups' terminology and reference architectures. Fork the reference implementation at github.com/onug/Fall26Demo and build on it, or record your own following the same beats. Full rules, the format table, the file naming pattern and the glossary are in the instructions:

https://github.com/onug/Fall26Demo/blob/main/docs/vendor-instructions.md

**How it is judged.** Differently from Dallas. There is no judging panel. Every video plays on loop in the Collaborative Center and the networking areas, companies that ask for a slot present in the theatre (Collaborative members get first priority for slots), and two scheduled Challenge Walks send attendees to your booth to see the submission with the people who built it. The on-site schedule for presentations, walks and office hours is still being built and will be posted in the Collaborative Slack.

**Rights.** You keep ownership; by entering you grant ONUG a worldwide, perpetual licence to host, post, display and translate your submission anywhere ONUG shows its work. The instructions have the exact wording. **Attending members vote in the Whova app, one ballot per lane. Best in Show is awarded per lane at the close.**
